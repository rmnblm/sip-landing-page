# Welcome to Sip

Thank you for being here.

Sip is your personal wine cellar and tasting journal — a calm place to track what you own, remember what you've poured, and notice what you love.

Add a few bottles, open one when the moment feels right, and jot down a tasting — even for a wine you don't own. Over time, your cellar and your notes become a story only you could write.

There's no feed and no noise. Just your wines, the way you like to keep them.

If you ever have a thought, a wish, or just want to say hello, I read every message.
