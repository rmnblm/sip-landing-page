Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 June 2026

Sip is built to be private by default. This policy explains what the app does — and, more importantly, what it doesn't do — with your information.

Your data stays yours

The wines, transactions, tastings, photos, and notes you create live on your device and, if you have iCloud enabled, sync through your own private iCloud account (Apple CloudKit). This data is not sent to the developer and the developer has no way to access it.

No tracking, no analytics, no ads

Sip contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. It does not build a profile of you and does not share data with advertisers or data brokers.

Purchases

Optional premium features are sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the transaction; the developer never receives your name, card, or payment details — only Apple's anonymous confirmation that a purchase is active.

Network requests

To keep currency conversions and release notes current, the app downloads a couple of small, public, static files (exchange rates and "What's New" text). These are ordinary anonymous web requests and carry no personal data beyond the standard technical information any web request includes (such as your IP address, visible only to the file host).
