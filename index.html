The Cellar

Every bottle, every format.

Your stock by size, with store and price, at a glance. Sip keeps the ledger so you always know what you own and where it is.

  • Multiple cellars, each with its own name and symbol
  • Drinking windows show what's ready tonight
  • Track opened bottles preserved with a Coravin
  • Purchases, deliveries, and consumption history

The Journal

A journal that remembers the night.

Photos first: a memory wall of everything you've tasted. Every tasting becomes a magazine-style spread with full-bleed photos, notes, ratings, and place.

  • Organize tastings with tags: trips, dinners, blind flights
  • Rate in stars, 20 points, or 100 points
  • Log wines you don't own, too

The Insights

Know your cellar.

What you drink, what it costs, what's ready tonight. Reports on consumption, composition, regions, and value, drawn from your own ledger.

  • Collection value and activity over time
  • Over 1,600 grape varieties to explore
  • Import and export as spreadsheets
  • Automatic iCloud sync between iPhone and iPad
